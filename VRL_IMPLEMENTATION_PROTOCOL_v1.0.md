## Rollout invariants

- **Reality → recipient → value → evidence → alternatives → economics → safe transition → decision → action.** The path is navigable, never forced.
- Role-specific recognition mirrors, with an optional technical/architectural reading mode; preserve distinct brands and professional tone.
- Claims use statuses `verified | observed | conceptual | proposed | unknown` and are traceable to dated sources. No invention of offerings, testimonials, prices, outcomes, or alleged third-party endorsements.
- Public content stays indexable and understandable without AI/JavaScript. Optional adaptive interface; not merely a chat bubble.
- Comparison includes the current approach, reasonable alternatives, and when appropriate doing nothing; no hiding our own weaknesses.
- Economic models expose assumptions and uncertainty. Time saved is not automatically salary saved. Do not assert unvalidated benefit.
- Commercial paths must name destination, offer, consent, and realistic next action; real delivery tests required.
- No cross-domain personal profiling by default. Central lead routing must preserve origin and user consent. Public AI knowledge excludes private sessions.
- AI is advisory; access control, rate limiting, source isolation, human consent, and data protection are mandatory.
- Honor the source document's 16 audit gates, change control, and 10 stages of implementation.

## Implementation sequence — per domain

0. Verify Cloudflare zone, canonical domain, repo, DNS, status (live/partial/prototype/planned/private), brand, locales; classify unreachable pages as **unverified**, not necessarily offline.
1. Audit content, claims and real lead delivery; produce `VRL_SITE_AUDIT`.
2. Develop stakeholder-role mirrors and role-driven questions; produce `ROLE_MIRROR_MATRIX`.
3. Map claims to sources, limits, objections and coherent threads; produce `THREADS + EVIDENCE`.
4. Publish indexable knowledge/FAQ and meaningful navigation before adding optional AI.
5. Pilot compact, accessible, optional Living Interface with deterministic fallback.
6. Add fair Comparison Engine, transparent Transformation Economics, Safe Transition modules only where suitable.
7. Build exportable Decision Brief and tested contact/lead route.
8. Test accessibility, mobile, privacy, LLM cost limits and real business outcomes; do not claim impact without evidence.
9. Reuse approved common components across remaining domains, preserving own positioning.
