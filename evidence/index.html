What counts as an outcome?

Published architecture is evidence of method, not evidence of return on investment. If a client authorizes anonymized reporting, each outcome should identify its baseline, period, measurement owner, method, comparison, limitations and degree of attribution.

Before / after

Define the observed baseline and the exact change.

Verified financial effect

Record net value after costs, rather than a speculative efficiency percentage.

Scope and responsibility

Distinguish architecture authored by RFS from actions taken by contractors and institutions.

Privacy and permission

Publish no identifiable customer detail or confidential system structure without explicit authorization.
