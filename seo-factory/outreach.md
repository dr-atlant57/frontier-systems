We develop competing architectural responses and test the critical assumptions before major investment. Our publicly accessible Problem Atlas shows representative work in enterprise AI adoption, integration after M&A, key-person decision continuity, disconnected corporate data, and AI agent governance.

If one of these problems appears in your portfolio or organization, we propose a **paid Architecture Discovery mission**: confirm the actual failure mechanism, compare three solution paths, agree on acceptance criteria, and define a staged experiment.
