A System Program is not a turnkey promise at a fixed total cost. Its boundaries, milestones, responsibilities, exclusions, decision gates and implementation partner budgets are agreed before a contract is signed.

DECISION GATES / NOT MORE CONSULTING HOURS

Reduce uncertainty before increasing commitment.

The goal is a sequence of decisions informed by evidence. Each gate can approve, revise, pause or stop the next step.

G0 / SCOPE

Value and boundaries

Clarify objectives, constraints, owner, expected benefit and excluded costs.

G1 / READINESS

Architecture and delivery

Approve architecture, vendor responsibilities, risks and measurable acceptance.

G2 / EVIDENCE

Test the working system

Review actual performance, anomalies, cost and the value hypothesis.

G3 / NEXT

Scale, adjust or pause

Decide on expansion only when risk and evidence justify it.

CLEAR RESPONSIBILITY BY DESIGN

The architect is not the construction crew.

RFS specializes in intellectual architecture, design assurance and architectural oversight. Execution, project management, financing and day-to-day operation require separately designated responsible parties.
