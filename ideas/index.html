FAQ

Before we begin

Is the $150 tier a full idea development engagement?

No. Idea Signal is a limited, paid first step: one challenge, one principal idea and a short rationale. Choose Idea Portfolio to explore 3–5 distinct directions, or a deeper stage for a developed solution or architecture.

Do we have to hire you for implementation?

No. You can purchase the agreed concept and work with any implementation team, subject to the usage rights specified in our agreement.

Do you guarantee economic success?

Every RFS concept starts by discovering and unlocking value: we examine hidden potential in the client's assets, knowledge, capabilities and opportunities, identify new ways to create value, and show how it could be realized. We then assess concrete benefits — additional revenue, reduced costs, time saved, avoided losses, greater productivity, new business opportunities and strategic advantage. Within the agreed scope, we explain the mechanisms, assumptions and ways to verify the expected effect. We do not guarantee automatic financial returns: results depend on implementation and market conditions. If the potential benefit does not justify the cost, we say so.

Who owns the ideas and the written concept?

Ownership, licence scope, exclusivity, confidentiality and any assignment of rights are defined in writing for each engagement. A package purchase does not automatically include unrestricted exclusive rights.

Can we start without a finished specification?

Yes. Begin with your goal, current obstacles and constraints. Clarifying the problem is part of the discovery process.
