Architectural unpacking of a mature enterprise system: from visible business symptoms to structural bottlenecks and intervention points.

An AI safety architecture built around active immune-system logic rather than a passive policy layer.

AI · SAFETY · RESILIENCE

HUMAN · COGNITION · DIGITAL TWIN
