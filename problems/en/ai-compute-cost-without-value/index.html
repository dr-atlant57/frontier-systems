RESEARCH DOSSIER · REQUIRES VALIDATION

AI infrastructure costs keep rising while business value stays unclear

More tokens and GPUs do not guarantee more accepted outcomes.

AI deployment adds model subscriptions, API calls, orchestration, storage and human verification. A cheaper model can produce more expensive mistakes; a more capable model may be uneconomic for routine work. The architectural unit of account should be the accepted business outcome.

Symptoms

  • Token and infrastructure spending increases without a corresponding benefit metric.
  • Different teams provision overlapping model services and monitoring.
  • Fallbacks and human rework make cheap inference unexpectedly expensive.

Potential root causes

  • Decisions are optimized for benchmarks or cost per token, not total outcome cost.
  • Workload routing and measurement are fragmented.
  • Energy use and idle hardware capacity are invisible in project economics.
01 / ARCHITECTURE

Alternative architectures

01

Task-based routing

Match model capability to risk and difficulty of each task.

02

End-to-end outcome accounting

Measure inference, orchestration, review and exception costs together.

03

Heterogeneous deployment

Compare cloud, edge and private compute against utilization, security and lifecycle cost.

Questions for the audit

  • What is the cost per independently accepted business outcome?
  • Which tasks require a stronger model, and which can be simpler?
  • What utilization and energy assumptions underpin the hardware plan?

Evidence to measure

  • Total cost per accepted outcome
  • Human rework time
  • Compute utilization and energy per successful task
02 / DISCOVERY

Proposed first deliverable

A measured workload-cost model and controlled optimization experiment.
