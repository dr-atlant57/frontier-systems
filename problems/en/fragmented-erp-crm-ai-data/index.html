Disconnected ERP, CRM and documents prevent reliable AI

The data exists. No system sees the whole picture.

ERP records, CRM histories and documents may all exist while a cross-department question still needs manual reconciliation. A model connection alone does not repair inconsistent entity definitions, stale records or access limits.

Symptoms

  • The same customer or contract appears under incompatible identifiers.
  • Teams reconcile conflicting figures by hand.
  • AI answers change with the department's selected data source.

Potential root causes

  • No governed semantic model or source provenance.
  • Interfaces expose fields without explaining business meaning.
  • Permissions were designed per application rather than end-to-end decision.

Alternative architectures

01

Semantic knowledge graph

Model identities, relationships, origins and known conflicts.

02

Federated access

Query authorized sources without copying everything to one central store.

03

Decision-centered data contracts

Specify required evidence, responsible owners and conflict resolution.

Questions for the audit

  • Which question requires the most manual cross-system work?
  • Who owns the authoritative definition of each key entity?
  • Which data cannot be combined due to contracts or law?

Evidence to measure

  • Time to a reconciled answer
  • Identifier conflicts per cycle
  • Answers with verifiable provenance
