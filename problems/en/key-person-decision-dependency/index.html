RESEARCH DOSSIER · REQUIRES VALIDATION

What if the business depends on one person's decisions?

The expert leaves. The reasoning leaves with them.

Instructions document routine actions but often omit why one alternative was chosen over another. When an expert leaves, successors lose the reasoning behind exceptions. A chatbot trained on old material is not automatically a reliable decision-maker.

Symptoms

  • Decisions stop when the founder or senior specialist is absent.
  • New employees can follow instructions but fail on exceptions.
  • Knowledge repositories capture documents but not decision criteria.

Potential root causes

  • Tacit reasoning was never elicited or independently validated.
  • Delegated authority and risk limits are undefined.
  • Knowledge transfer is measured by files collected, not decisions reproduced.
01 / ARCHITECTURE

Alternative architectures

01

Decision-case library

Interview experts and document decisions, rejected options and exceptions.

02

Validated Decision DNA

Test decision rules on held-out cases and track uncertainty and drift.

03

Supervised executive twin

Recommend permitted actions and refer ambiguous cases to people.

Questions for the audit

  • Which decisions depend on a single person today?
  • Do you have permission to use the underlying records and chats?
  • Which decisions must remain human-only?

Evidence to measure

  • Agreement on held-out decision cases
  • Time to resolve exceptional cases
  • Rate of human escalation
